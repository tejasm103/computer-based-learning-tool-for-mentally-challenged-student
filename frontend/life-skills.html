Daily Hygiene & Self-Care Checklist

Learn the proper steps for clean habits, healthy body care, and screen time balance.

20-Second Proper Handwashing Steps:

  1. Wet hands with clean, running water.
  2. Apply enough soap to cover all hand surfaces.
  3. Rub palms together in circular motions.
  4. Rub back of each hand with fingers interlaced.
  5. Clean thumbs and scrub under fingernails.
  6. Rinse thoroughly with clean water.
  7. Dry completely with a clean towel.

20-20-20 Eye Strain Rule:

Every 20 minutes spent looking at a computer or phone screen, look away at an object at least 20 feet away for at least 20 seconds.
