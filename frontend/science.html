Plant Photosynthesis & Respiration Lab

Adjust sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide to see how plants generate oxygen and food.

Production Rates:

Oxygen (O₂):
65%
Glucose (Sugar):
60%
Scientific Formula: 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + Sunlight ➔ C₆H₁₂O₆ (Glucose) + 6O₂ (Oxygen).

Leaves contain a green pigment called chlorophyll that captures sunlight energy to transform water from the soil and carbon dioxide from the air into food!

Pendulum & Gravity Lab

Discover how gravity and string length affect the pendulum oscillation period.

Physics Law: Period T = 2π √(L / g). Notice that string length and gravity determine the period, not the mass of the bob!
